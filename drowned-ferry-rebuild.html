Phase 1 — Canonical Master Ferry NEXT

Establish one exterior silhouette and structural truth model: hull, superstructure, deck stack, bow/stern orientation, major openings and overall real-world scale. No interior hero scenes yet.

Phase 2 — Sectional Breakdown

Divide the master vessel into reusable bow, forward-midship, central-midship, aft-midship, stern and upper-superstructure exterior sections. Lock section seams and deck elevations.

Phase 3 — Shared Ferry Structural Kit

Create reusable ribs, shell plates, bulkheads, decks, stairs, catwalks, railings, hatches, doors, supports, ducting and damage variants.

Phase 4 — Internal Scene Slots

Only after the exterior/deck stack is approved: assign internal scene zones to exact locations in the vessel. Each room/POI inherits the master ferry's dimensions, deck elevation, access points and adjacent spaces.

Phase 5 — Modular Internal Scenes

Every scene is assembled from multiple reusable components. No one-piece room meshes. Meshy image-to-3D is used for visible hero components; Blender provides cleanup, exact scale, modular assembly and precision gameplay backbones where required.

Phase 6 — Unreal Assembly / QA

Import approved modules, assemble sectional exterior first, then interiors. Validate walking clearance, collision, pivots, scale, section seams, route continuity and performance before lookdev.

Permanent Build Rules

  1. Exterior master vessel comes before interior POIs.
  2. One scene is always built from several components.
  3. No whole-room Meshy generation and no non-uniform stretching to force a scene into scale.
  4. Normal ferry traversal is walking unless a specific area is explicitly designated as a swim section.
  5. 1.8 m player reference; normal walking review target at least 1.2 m clear width × 2.2 m clear height.
  6. Geometry and scale first. Shaders/material polish are a later pass.
  7. No new numbered interior structure until the master ferry section/deck location is approved.
