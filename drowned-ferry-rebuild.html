Locked Build Rules

  1. Every Ferry scene is assembled from multiple reusable components; no one-piece room/POI mesh.
  2. Default visible-asset workflow is reference → Meshy → Blender cleanup/uniform scale → modular assembly → public review → Game Director approval → Unreal.
  3. Explicit exception: silhouette-defining primary geometry may be designated as precision-Blender vessel truth when Meshy cannot preserve the approved shape; localized Meshy detail is then added only after the clean form passes.
  4. Precision Blender geometry may also support traversal/collision underneath visible Meshy detail.
  5. Do not non-uniformly stretch a failed Meshy result; regenerate or split it.
  6. Default traversal is walking unless a specific area is explicitly approved as swim-only.
  7. Player reference is 1.8 m; ordinary walking target is at least 1.2 m clear width × 2.2 m clear height.
  8. Geometry and scale precede shaders/material polish.
